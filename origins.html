Origins

1981 – 1996

From BITNET to Broadband

The Origins of Singapore's Internet

Singapore's internet story begins in the lecture halls and laboratories of the National University of Singapore, where the first connections to global networks laid the foundation for a digital revolution.

The Beginning

The Academic Genesis

1981

National Computer Board

Singapore's digital journey officially began on 1 September 1981 with the establishment of the National Computer Board (NCB). Tasked with spearheading the nation's computerisation, the NCB launched the Civil Service Computerisation Programme, digitising government services and laying the groundwork for Singapore's future as a technology leader.

1987

NUS Connects to BITNET

In January 1987, the National University of Singapore achieved a historic milestone by becoming the first permanent BITNET node in Southeast Asia outside Japan. This connection allowed NUS researchers to communicate directly with over 600 universities worldwide.

1990

First Internet Connection

By 1990, NUS had secured Singapore's first full connection to the global Internet, opening doors to unprecedented research collaboration and information access. The NUSNET campus network, officially launched on 11 April 1991, connected the university to more than 3,000 institutions worldwide via fibre-optic cable.

The Pioneers

Singapore's First ISPs

The companies that brought the Internet to the public

🌐

TechNet

1991

Established by the National Science and Technology Board (NSTB), TechNet was Singapore's first Internet service provider, serving the research and education community.

📱

SingNet

1994

Launched by Singtel in July 1994, SingNet brought commercial Internet access to the Singapore public for the first time.

📈

Pacific Internet

1995

Formed when Sembawang Media purchased TechNet, Pacific Internet became a major ISP and later the first Asian ISP to list on NASDAQ.

⚡

Cyberway

1996

Singapore's third major ISP, later acquired by StarHub in 1999, expanding competition in the consumer market.
