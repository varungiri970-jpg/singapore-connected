Infrastructure

Fibre for Everyone

2010

Next Generation National Broadband Network

In August 2010, Singapore launched its Next Generation National Broadband Network (NGNBN), an all-fibre network delivering symmetrical speeds of 1 Gbps to homes and businesses. This infrastructure placed Singapore among the global leaders in broadband connectivity and enabled the digital services that would follow.

2024

10 Gbps Upgrade

In February 2024, IMDA announced a S$100 million investment to upgrade the nation's broadband network to 10 Gbps speeds — ensuring that Singapore remains at the forefront of global connectivity.

Smart Nation Launch

24 November 2014

PM Lee Hsien Loong launched the Smart Nation vision, a national strategy to harness technology for better living.

🏛

GovTech

October 2016

The Government Technology Agency was formed to build digital capabilities and drive the Smart Nation agenda.

📋

SNDGO

1 May 2017

The Smart Nation & Digital Government Office was established under the PMO to coordinate strategy.

💳

PayNow

10 July 2017

Singapore's instant peer-to-peer fund transfer system was launched, revolutionising digital payments.

🤖

National AI Strategy

13 November 2019

Five National AI Projects were announced to position Singapore as a global AI leader.

📡

5G

June 2020

Singtel and a StarHub-M1 joint venture were awarded 5G licences, beginning Singapore's 5G rollout.

✨

Smart Nation 2.0

1 October 2024

PM Lawrence Wong launched the refreshed Smart Nation vision: Trust, Growth, Community.
