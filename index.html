About This Project

Why Singapore's Internet Journey Matters

Singapore's transformation from a small island nation to a global digital powerhouse is one of the most remarkable stories in technology history. In just four decades, the country built a world-class digital infrastructure that now supports one of the highest internet penetration rates and fastest broadband speeds on the planet.

This website traces that journey — from the first BITNET connection at NUS in 1987, through the commercial internet boom of the 1990s, the broadband revolution of the 2000s, and the Smart Nation vision that continues to shape Singapore's digital future today.
