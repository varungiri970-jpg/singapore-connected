Pioneering Innovation

World's First Nationwide Broadband

1997

Singapore ONE

On 9 June 1997, Prime Minister Goh Chok Tong launched Singapore ONE (One Network for Everyone) — the world's first nationwide broadband multimedia network. Operating at speeds up to 155 Mbps, the network connected schools, libraries, government agencies, and homes, delivering services like video-on-demand, tele-education, and tele-medicine.

This pioneering infrastructure demonstrated Singapore's commitment to becoming a leader in the digital economy and set the stage for the broadband revolution that followed.

Strategic Planning

Strategic Masterplans

How Singapore planned its digital future

📋

Infocomm 21

2000

Launched in December 2000, Infocomm 21 set a five-year vision to transform Singapore into a global Infocomm Capital.

🆔

Singpass

2003

Introduced in March 2003, Singapore's national digital identity system enabled citizens to access government e-services securely.

🧠

iN2015

2006

A S$3.2 billion, 10-year masterplan launched in June 2006 to transform Singapore into an Intelligent Nation.

📶

Wireless@SG

2006

Launched in December 2006, Singapore became one of the first countries to offer nationwide free Wi-Fi to citizens and visitors.
